Help with subscriptions, free trials, and renewal reminders.

Getting started

Add a service from the catalog or create a custom subscription. Enter its price, currency, billing cycle, and next billing date, then save. Dayby uses the information you enter to estimate recurring spending and show upcoming renewals.

For a free trial, record the trial end date and check the first payment details. Open a subscription to update its information when your plan changes.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

  • Allow notifications for Dayby in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Check that notifications are enabled in Dayby's Settings and reminders are enabled for the subscription.
  • Confirm the renewal or trial date is correct and still in the future.
  • Check whether Focus or Scheduled Summary is delaying or hiding notifications.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Keep important billing dates in your own records as well.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Dayby is a manual tracker. Editing or deleting a record in Dayby does not cancel the underlying subscription or stop charges. Cancel with the service provider, or through Apple's subscription settings if you subscribed through the App Store.

Why do spending totals look different from my bill?

Monthly and annual totals are estimates based on the prices and billing cycles you enter. Currency conversions may differ from your bank or provider's exchange rate. Check the original service bill for the exact amount and update your Dayby record as needed.

Where is my subscription data stored?

Your subscription records are stored on your device. Dayby does not currently provide an account or cloud synchronization for those records. Deleting the app can remove its local data, so avoid reinstalling as a troubleshooting step unless you are prepared to recreate your records.

Can I use Dayby without an account?

Yes. Core tracking does not require signing in or connecting a bank account. An internet connection is needed for catalog searches and exchange-rate updates. If a service is missing from the catalog, add a custom subscription.
