Privacy Policy

What Ridebook stores, what it syncs, and the choices you have.

Effective September 30, 2026

In short: Ridebook keeps your journal on your device by default. If you sign in and use Ridebook Plus sync, supported journal data and selected photos are sent to our cloud provider so they can be available on your other devices. Apple and RevenueCat process information needed for sign-in and subscriptions.

1. Scope and who we are

This policy applies to the Ridebook mobile app. “Ridebook,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the app’s developer. This policy explains what information is handled, why it is used, and your choices.

2. Information stored on your device

Ridebook stores the information you add to your journal in a local database on your device. This may include route names and details, commute start and end times, activities, journal entries, thoughts and follow-ups, tags, preferences, profile settings, and related dates. Photos you choose are copied into app storage and linked to the relevant journal entry. This information remains local unless you choose to use account sync.

3. Apple sign-in and account information

If you choose to connect an account, Ridebook uses Sign in with Apple through Supabase Authentication. Apple may provide a stable account identifier and, depending on your Apple settings, your name or email address (including a private relay address). We use the account identifier to associate your synced data with your Ridebook account. Apple and Supabase process sign-in information under their own privacy practices.

4. Optional cloud sync and photos

Ridebook Plus enables sync for signed-in users. When sync is active, Ridebook sends supported journal records and profile settings to Supabase so they can be stored with your account and synchronized between your devices. This can include routes, commute dates, journal text, thoughts, activity records, and related metadata. Photos associated with synced entries may be uploaded to Supabase Storage. The app uses your account identifier to associate these records and files with your account.

Sync requires an internet connection and an active Plus entitlement. If you do not sign in or use sync, your journal remains on your device and is not uploaded by Ridebook’s sync feature. Sync availability is not a substitute for keeping an export or backup.

5. Subscriptions and purchase information

If you purchase or restore Ridebook Plus, Apple processes payment. RevenueCat helps us retrieve subscription status, validate entitlements, and manage purchase records. The app may associate purchase status with your Ridebook app account so that Plus features work across your account. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchase-related information under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Photos and device permissions

You choose whether to add a photo using the device’s photo picker. Ridebook does not need access to your full photo library; it handles the photo you select. Selected photos are stored locally and, if you use Plus sync, may be uploaded as described above. Ridebook does not collect your precise location or track your movements through GPS.

7. Analytics and tracking

Ridebook does not currently include a product analytics or advertising tracking service. The RevenueCat SDK may communicate with RevenueCat to provide subscription functionality, and Apple or Supabase may receive technical information as part of their services. We do not use journal content for advertising.

8. How we use and share information

We use information to provide local journaling, account sign-in, sync, photo backup, subscription access, customer support, security, and legal compliance. We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to operate features you choose, including:

  • Apple, for sign-in, App Store billing, and subscription management;
  • Supabase, for optional account authentication, database sync, and photo storage; and
  • RevenueCat, for subscription status and purchase management.

We may also disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, our rights, or the service. Providers may process information in countries other than your own. See Apple Privacy, Supabase Privacy, and RevenueCat Privacy for their policies.

9. Retention, deletion, and export

Local journal information remains on your device until you delete it, clear Ridebook’s data, or uninstall the app. Synced information remains in Supabase while associated with your account and as needed to provide sync, maintain security, or comply with law. Deleting the app from a device does not by itself delete cloud-synced information or cancel an App Store subscription.

You can export your journal from Ridebook’s Settings. To request deletion of your Ridebook account and associated cloud data, or to ask us to locate or correct account information, contact us at the address below. We may ask for information to verify your request. We will handle requests subject to applicable law and any information we must retain for legal, security, or transaction records.

10. Your choices

  • Use Ridebook locally without connecting an account or enabling sync.
  • Choose which photos to add, and remove photos or entries in the app.
  • Export your journal from Settings.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
  • Contact us to request access to or deletion of account information.

11. Security

We and our service providers use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your device and Apple account and keep an export of important journal information.

12. Children’s privacy

Ridebook is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Ridebook, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Ridebook or our practices change. We will publish any revised policy here and update the effective date. Where appropriate, we may also notify you in the app.
