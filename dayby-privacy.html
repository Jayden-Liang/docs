Privacy Policy

How Dayby handles information when you track subscriptions and renewal dates.

Effective September 15, 2026

In short: Dayby does not require an account. Your subscription list is stored on your device. We use limited analytics to improve the app, and catalog searches are sent to the service needed to return search results.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Dayby ("Dayby," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and retains information when you use the Dayby mobile application.

2. Information stored on your device

Information you enter about your subscriptions is stored in the app's local database on your device. This may include service names, prices, currencies, billing cycles and dates, categories, notes, trial details, reminder settings, status, website links, and app icons. App preferences and locally scheduled notification information are also stored on your device.

Dayby does not currently provide user accounts or cloud synchronization for this subscription data, and we do not receive a copy of your complete local subscription database.

3. Information transmitted from the app

Catalog searches

When you search for a service or app, your search query is sent to our catalog provider, Supabase, and may be sent to Apple's iTunes Search API so matching results can be returned. These providers may receive technical information normally sent with an internet request, such as your IP address, device or browser information, and request time. When an Apple result is selected, public app-catalog details may be added to our shared catalog; your private subscription details are not included.

Product analytics

Release builds may send limited product-usage events to PostHog. These events help us understand app navigation, feature use, searches, reminders, errors, and subscription workflows. Depending on the action, analytics may include:

  • the service name associated with an added or renamed subscription;
  • billing-cycle, trial, reminder, and subscription-status categories;
  • country and currency preferences;
  • screens viewed, buttons or features used, result counts, and non-content error categories; and
  • app, device, operating-system, network, and pseudonymous identifier information provided automatically by the analytics service.

We have disabled session replay and person profiles in our PostHog configuration. We do not intentionally send subscription prices, billing dates, notes, email addresses, or names of users as analytics properties. Please avoid entering personal or sensitive information as a custom service name.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Dayby uses your device's notification system to schedule renewal and trial reminders locally. You can change notification permission in your device settings.

4. How we use information

We use information to operate the app, return catalog search results, schedule requested reminders, understand feature usage, diagnose failures, improve performance and usability, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable law.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We disclose information only as described here:

  • PostHog processes product analytics on our behalf.
  • Supabase hosts and serves our app catalog and receives catalog search requests.
  • Apple may process search requests made through the iTunes Search API and handles device notifications and App Store distribution.
  • We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or complete a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Learn more at PostHog Privacy, Supabase Privacy, and Apple Privacy.

6. Retention and deletion

Your locally stored subscription data remains on your device until you delete individual records, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Information held by service providers is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to their retention settings, legal obligations, and security requirements.

You can delete local subscription records within Dayby. To request deletion of analytics information associated with your device, or to ask a privacy question, email us using the address below. We may need information from you to locate and verify the relevant record.

7. Your choices

  • You can use the core app without creating an account.
  • You can deny or revoke notification permission in device settings.
  • You can avoid catalog search and create a custom subscription manually.
  • You can delete subscriptions in the app or uninstall Dayby to remove locally stored app data.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Service providers may process information in countries other than yours, where privacy laws may differ.

9. Children's privacy

Dayby is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Dayby changes. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app when appropriate.
